Payouts are not profit

Payout figures shown are gross payouts, not profit. Profit is always lower than payouts, and can be zero or negative.

Prop firms and funded accounts

Many prop firm ("funded trader") accounts are simulated accounts. The firm sets the rules, decides whether you pass an evaluation, and decides whether, when and how much it pays out. Firms can change their rules, refuse or delay payouts, close accounts or stop operating. Evaluation fees are usually not refundable. We are not affiliated with, and do not endorse, any prop firm, broker or trading platform named or shown on this Site unless we clearly say so. If we ever earn a commission or other benefit when you sign up with a firm, broker or tool we mention, we will tell you.

Trading bots and automated trading

  • Bots and indicators can malfunction and lose money. They can have bugs, stop working, or place unintended trades because of platform, broker, data, internet or server problems.
  • Backtests are not live results. A bot can behave very differently in live markets than in a backtest or on a demo account, because of slippage, spreads, commissions, latency, liquidity and changing market conditions. Backtested results are hypothetical (see below).
  • Prop firm rules: a misconfigured bot can break a prop firm's rules (for example daily loss or drawdown limits) and cost you an evaluation or a funded account. Some prop firms prohibit or restrict automated trading or expert advisors. Check your firm's current rules before you run any bot.
  • You are solely responsible for configuring, testing, monitoring and running any bot, the risk settings you choose, and every trade it places.
  • There is no guarantee that any bot will be profitable, that you will pass any evaluation or challenge, or that you will get funded or receive a payout.

Trading is risky

Trading forex, futures, stocks, options, CFDs, crypto and other markets involves substantial risk of loss. Leverage can work against you as well as for you, and with some accounts you can lose more than you put in. Prices can move fast and without warning. Only trade with money you can afford to lose, and never borrow money, use money you need for bills, or take on debt to trade or to buy evaluations.

Past performance, whether actual, backtested or simulated, is not indicative of future results.

What happens for you depends on your effort, time, discipline, skills, experience, the money you have available, how you set up and run any bot, the rules of the firm or broker you use, and market conditions. Many of these are outside your control and ours.
